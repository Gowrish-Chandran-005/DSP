## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a calm, mindful sanctuary engineered specifically for students navigating rigorous academic demands. Instead of sterile hospital metrics or alarming performance dashboards, the interface evokes a restorative greenhouse: tranquil, protective, organic, and non-judgmental.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Restorative & Reassuring:** Prioritizes decompression over vigilance. Metrics act as gentle reflections rather than clinical verdicts.
- **Nurturing & Human-Centric:** Avoids panic-inducing indicators, high-contrast alerts, or harsh warning cues. Elevated burnout markers are treated with soft amber awareness, inviting reflection rather than provoking stress.
- **Subtle Organic Vitality:** Employs breathing status rhythms, soft botanical gradients, and peaceful micro-movements that promote parasympathetic recovery.

### Visual Aesthetic
The interface synthesizes organic minimalism with airy glassmorphism. Surfaces emulate frosted morning glass overlooking misty foliage: light-permeable panes, translucent multi-layered depth, soft liquid outlines, and tactile yet weightless card constructs.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from Japanese forest bathing (*shinrin-yoku*), botanical glasshouses, and morning dew. The entire chromatic architecture avoids jarring contrasts and strictly forbids crimson or aggressive scarlet tones, even under critical thresholds.

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Forest Green (`#14532D`):** The grounding anchor. Used for primary call-to-actions, prominent typographic titles, active tab highlights, and structural emphasis. Communicates stability and deep tranquility.
- **Sage (`#CFEBDD`):** Mid-tone botanical buffer. Drives tinted secondary surfaces, active states, soft borders, and filled chips.
- **Light Aqua (`#D8F3F0`):** Refreshing, clear accent. Used for atmospheric gradient shifts, background orb accents, and recovery state signifiers.
- **Muted Grey-Green Neutral (`#5B6F65`):** Secondary metadata, inactive states, axis lines, and supportive editorial body text. Ensures legible contrast without pure black severity.

### Canvas & Backgrounds
- **Mint Atmosphere (`#E9F7EF`):** The primary page canvas. A soft, warm-mint wash that replaces pure cold white to shield user eyes during late-night study sessions.
- **Deep Green-Black Body (`#10261B`):** Selected exclusively for high-clarity reading across headings and core body paragraphs. Softer and less straining than `#000000`.

### Alert & Notice Architecture
- **Soft Warm Amber (`#E09B4C`):** Replaces destructive red across the system. Denotes elevated exhaustion, depleted efficacy, or recommended rest cycles. Framed with gentle amber tinted washes (`#FCF6ED`) and soft glowing edges.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve an open, contemporary, and warmly empathetic tone. The wide geometric counters, friendly terminal curves, and balanced x-height eliminate technical friction and clinical sterility.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered with tighter letter spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and soft semi-bold or bold weights. Used for mindful greeting moments, metric summaries, and reflective check-in prompts.
- **Body Text:** Uses generous line heights (`1.57` to `1.62`) and muted contrast (`#10261B` for focus; `#5B6F65` for context) to ensure readability without ocular strain during prolonged review.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Slight tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) applied to small capsule titles, metric categories (e.g., Emotional Exhaustion, Cynicism, Academic Efficacy), and navigation elements. Never rendered in all-caps shouting; prefer Title Case or Sentence Case.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on fluid spatial harmony and generous breathing margins. Visual elements avoid density and crowded columns, allowing each wellness metric and chart to occupy distinct, unhurried zones.

### Spatial Rhythm & Grid Structure
- **Mobile (Base to 639px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins and gutters. Cards take 100% column width to maximize touch comfort.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Burnout dimensions display in paired modular blocks.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` centered canvas, utilizing `3rem` (48px) margins and `2rem` (32px) gutters. Complex trend charts expand across 8 columns, with supportive reflection prompts across 4 columns.

### Density & Breathing Room
Content cards enforce generous internal padding (`space-md` on mobile, `space-lg` on desktop) ensuring data points never crowd card edges. Vertical section gaps rely on `space-xl` (48px) to establish paced cognitive intake.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through luminous multi-layered glassmorphism and ultra-diffused atmospheric illumination. Traditional opaque drop shadows and harsh outlines are excluded.

### The Glass Surface Architecture
Panels mimic semi-opaque frosted glass hovering over soft, slow-moving botanical color fields:
- **Base Ambient Canvas:** Tinted fluid background (`#E9F7EF`) overlaid with slow-drifting, deeply blurred organic blurs of Sage (`#CFEBDD`) and Light Aqua (`#D8F3F0`).
- **Surface Tier 1 (Cards & Modules):** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.68)`, backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`. Outlined with a soft translucent highlight: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Floating Popovers & Action Sheets):** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.

### Shadow & Glow Physics
Shadows are ultra-diffused, tinted with the deep botanical base rather than neutral black:
- **Default Floating Elevation:** `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.03)`.
- **Hover & Active Elevation:** `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.10), 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04)`.
- **Warm Notice Glow:** `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(224, 155, 76, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The design system relies on welcoming, pill-shaped geometry. Hard corners and sharp angular silhouettes are absent to reduce cognitive friction and promote visual serenity.

### Corner Radius System
- **Main Metric Panels & Glass Cards:** Explicitly crafted with a continuous organic radius between `28px` and `32px` (`rounded-xl` to custom `32px`).
- **Interactive Buttons & Chips:** Full pill contour (`9999px`) for seamless tactile invitation.
- **Input Fields & Modal Sheets:** `20px` to `24px` rounded profiles that echo the softness of the primary card containers.
- **Status Indicators & Avatars:** Perfect circles (`50%` / circular geometry).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Forest Green (`#14532D`) background with pure white typography, full pill radius, and subtle botanical inner glow. Hover triggers a gentle upward lift and deep glow (`rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Glass Button:** Translucent white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, text `#14532D`.
- **Quiet / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#5B6F65`, hover transition to `rgba(207, 235, 221, 0.3)`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Selection Chips:** Pill-shaped, `padding: 0.5rem 1.25rem`. Inactive state sits on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)` with `#5B6F65` text. Active state adopts Sage green (`#CFEBDD`) fill with `#14532D` label and gentle inset shadow.

### Input Fields
- Built on translucent rounded troughs (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`), border `1px solid rgba(207, 235, 221, 0.6)`. Active focus introduces an organic halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(207, 235, 221, 0.45)`) and transitions background opacity to `0.85`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular organic toggles with soft Spring easing. Selected checkboxes showcase a deep forest green wash with a soft white check glyph; unselected states use a soft sage ring.

### Cards & Metric Containers
- Glassmorphic panels with `28px` to `32px` corner radii. Internal padding calibrated to `2rem`. Feature dual-surface hierarchy: nested metrics rest within inset translucent pods (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`).

### Specialized System Components
- **Breathing Status Orbs:** Dynamic, multi-layered radial gradient spheres representing real-time burnout balance (Exhaustion, Cynicism, Efficacy). Animated with a gentle 4-second sinusoidal expansion and contraction pulse (*inhale/exhale* rhythm). Orbs transition fluidly between Light Aqua, Sage, and Soft Warm Amber based on current equilibrium.
- **Serene Trend Line Charts:** Minimalist linear curves rendered with anti-aliased 2.5px paths. No harsh grid lines; relies solely on soft horizontal reference levels at 20% opacity. The area beneath the curve carries a soft vertical gradient falling from `rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.12)` down to `rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.0)`. Interactive data points reveal frosted glass tooltip capsules with gentle spring dynamics.
- **Mindful Notice Banners:** For elevated burnout markers. Tinted with soft amber glass (`rgba(252, 246, 237, 0.85)`), amber-tinted border (`#E09B4C` at 30% opacity), and deep green-black supportive copy, avoiding alarming badge icons in favor of calm, actionable reflection questions.
